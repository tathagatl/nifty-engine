## How this screen was run (read before acting)

- Screener.in's query screen could not be fetched from this environment, so a candidate list of 32 well-known high-ROCE / low-debt Nifty 500 names was checked one by one on Screener.in company pages. Stocks outside that list were **not** screened, so this is not an exhaustive scan of all 500.
- **ROCE**: plain mean of FY22–FY26 annual ROCE (Screener Ratios table). **D/E**: latest Borrowings ÷ (Equity + Reserves). **Growth**: Screener "Compounded Sales/Profit Growth 5 Years". **CFO**: positive years in FY22–FY26. Banks/NBFC: ROA computed as profit ÷ average total assets; gross NPA from Q2 FY27 releases.
- **Valuation**: Screener hides its median P/E, so the comparison uses **Trendlyne's stated 5-year average P/E** (an average, not a median; basis not labelled). Fallback: **PEG = TTM P/E ÷ 5Y profit CAGR**. Where neither was available the stock was not passed.
- **Pledge**: no source this run showed pledge % (Screener confirmed zero only for KEI). The pledge rule is **unverified** for the others — check the NSE shareholding pattern before buying.
