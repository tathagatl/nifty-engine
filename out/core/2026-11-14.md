## Correction to last week

Last week's report passed **BAJFINANCE**. Read literally, the rulebook only swaps ROCE and debt for ROA and NPA when screening banks and NBFCs. The cash-flow rule still applies to them. Bajaj Finance had **negative operating cash flow in 0 of 5 years** (FY22–FY26), so it fails that rule. Its operating cash flow is normally negative because a lender's new loans count as operating outflows.

HDFCBANK (4 of 5 years positive) and ICICIBANK (4 of 5) still pass. If you'd rather not apply the cash-flow rule to lenders, say so and BAJFINANCE goes back in. It would rank about 7th (P/E 31.7 vs 5-year average 39.7, −20%).

## How this screen was run (read before acting)

- **Coverage:** Screener's query screen was blocked again (permission timeout), so candidates were checked one by one on Screener company pages. This is not an exhaustive scan of all 500 stocks.
- **ROCE:** plain mean of FY22–FY26 annual ROCE (CY21–CY25 for CRISIL).
- **D/E:** Sep-2026 borrowings ÷ (equity + reserves).
- **Growth:** Screener "Compounded Sales/Profit Growth 5 Years".
- **Valuation:** Trendlyne's stated "5 year PE average". This is an average, not the median the rule asks for. For some stocks it doesn't match Trendlyne's own 2-year table; those cases are flagged. Where no average was available, PEG (TTM P/E ÷ 5Y profit CAGR) was used.
- **Pledge:** verified at ~0% only for KEI, ABBOTINDIA, BAJFINANCE, COALINDIA and SUNPHARMA. HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK, CAMS, MCX and BSE have no promoter. **For the others, pledge is unverified.** Check the NSE shareholding pattern before buying.

### Notes

1. **PAGEIND:** Jockey licensee in India, selling innerwear and athleisure.
   - Why it passes: ROCE 58%, low debt, steady promoter holding at 42.89%, and 40% below its 5-year average P/E.
   - Risk: sales growth only 13%. Q1 FY27 profit was −4% and Q2 +4%. Operating cash flow was slightly negative in FY23 (−₹2 Cr). Screener's consolidated page was blank, so the standalone figures were used.
2. **HAVELLS:** electricals and appliances (fans, switches, cables, Lloyd ACs).
   - Why it passes: ROCE 25%, almost no debt, 38% below its 5-year average P/E.
   - Risk: profit growth of 11% is close to the 10% floor. Q1 FY27 profit was −17% YoY; Q2 was +10%, so this is not two quarters down. Trendlyne page dated 5-Nov. PEG is 3.9, so it passes on the P/E-history test only.
3. **PIDILITIND:** adhesives and construction chemicals (Fevicol).
   - Why it passes: ROCE 28%, debt-free, profit up YoY in each of the last 4 quarters.
   - Risk: still 58× earnings. Trendlyne's 2-year average (~67) is far below its stated 5-year figure (88.5), though the stock is below both. Promoter holding is drifting down slightly (69.32 → 69.19%).
4. **HDFCBANK:** India's largest private bank.
   - Why it passes: ROA 1.9%, GNPA 1.11%, 33% below its 5-year average P/E.
   - Risk: management turnover. Anup Bagchi became MD & CEO on 27-Oct, and Deputy MD Kaizad Bharucha leaves on 31-Dec. Q2 standalone PAT grew only 4.7%. Growth figures are inflated by the HDFC merger.
5. **ABBOTINDIA:** Indian arm of Abbott (branded generics).
   - Why it passes: ROCE 43%, debt-free, 0% pledge, promoter flat at 74.99%.
   - Risk: sales growth exactly at the 10% floor. The Trendlyne page is stale (28-Oct). One share costs ~₹26,440, which won't fit a ₹3,300 tranche.
6. **KEI:** wires and EHV/HT/LT cables.
   - Why it passes: ROCE 24%, profit CAGR 27%, zero pledge, profit up every quarter.
   - Risk: ROCE has slipped (27 → 20%), and FY25 operating cash flow was −₹32 Cr. Same copper and capex exposure as POLYCAB.
7. **CRISIL:** ratings and research business, majority-owned by S&P Global.
   - Why it passes: ROCE 37%, low debt, promoter flat at 66.64%, 27% below its 5-year average P/E.
   - Risk: growth of 13% / 16% is moderate, and PEG is 2.2, so it passes on the P/E-history test only. Pledge unverified.
8. **POLYCAB:** wires, cables and FMEG (fast-moving electrical goods).
   - Why it passes: ROCE 28%, sales CAGR 27%, Sep-quarter profit +43%.
   - Risk: same sector exposure as KEI. Promoter holding is down from 63% to 61.4% over two years but flat for the last 4 quarters.
9. **NESTLEIND:** packaged foods (Maggi, Nescafé).
   - Why it passes: very high ROCE, near-zero debt, profit up in every one of the last 4 quarters.
   - Risk: ROCE is falling (169 → 84%). The change of financial year means the 5-year periods aren't like-for-like. Growth is just over 10%.
10. **SOLARINDS:** industrial explosives and defence munitions.
    - Why it passes: ROCE 34%, profit CAGR 45%.
    - The 3-Nov auditor change is **confirmed as a term-end rotation**, not a resignation: SRBC finished its second 5-year term, and Price Waterhouse joins from the 2027 AGM.
    - Risk: 77× earnings; the 5-year average is on a standalone basis while the P/E is consolidated. FY26 operating cash flow fell to ₹621 Cr.
